## Product Overview
**One-liner:** WebCrew is an AI front office for local businesses — it answers every call 24/7, qualifies and books leads, and follows up automatically, so missed calls stop becoming lost revenue.
**What it does:** Builds a custom, high-performance website overnight (avg. 97/100 PageSpeed) and pairs it with an AI receptionist (Gemini Live voice) that answers inbound calls, texts, and web-widget chats 24/7 — qualifying callers, booking real appointments via Cal.com, sending an instant SMS/email lead alert, texting back anyone who couldn't get through, and following up automatically at day 3 and day 10 so a warm lead never goes cold. Also runs the ongoing local-marketing layer: weekly Google Business Profile posts, automatic Google review replies, and a monthly traffic/ranking report.
**Product category:** AI answering / AI front-office software for local service businesses (adjacent to missed-call-text-back and local-SEO/reputation tools).
**Product type:** SaaS (website + AI voice/SMS agent + local marketing automation, bundled as one product, not sold separately).
**Business model:** Flat-rate subscription. $0 setup fee (normally $499, waived for every current customer), 30-day free trial (card required, no charge until trial ends), then $297/month flat — one plan, everything included, no tiers or add-ons.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Local, owner-operated service businesses in the US — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, cleaning, auto services, salons, med spas/spas, contractors, general home services. Small teams (often solo operators or small crews) where the owner or a single front-desk person is also doing the actual work.
**Decision-makers:** The owner/operator directly — not a marketing department. Non-technical buyer.
**Primary use case:** Stop losing jobs to a missed call. The owner is on a job, driving, or with a customer; the phone rings; it goes to voicemail; the caller hangs up and calls the next business on Google instead.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Never let an inbound call go unanswered, at any hour
- Turn an inbound call/text into a booked, confirmed appointment without the owner touching it
- Keep showing up and looking credible in local search/Google Business Profile without spending time on it
**Use cases:**
- After-hours and weekend calls that would otherwise hit voicemail
- Owner mid-job/driving, can't pick up
- Business with no website yet (site built and live same day, "wake up to a text with the link")
- Business with an outdated/slow existing site (revenue-recovery angle: no-show rate, missed-call rate)

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Every unanswered call is a customer who books with a competitor instead — and the owner has no way to be reachable 24/7 while also doing the physical work of the business.
**Why alternatives fall short:** Voicemail converts near-zero callers (nobody leaves a message for a business they haven't hired yet). Human answering services (Ruby, Smith.ai-style) are expensive per-minute/per-call and don't build or maintain the website or do the local-marketing layer. DIY tools (a website alone, or a text-back tool alone) solve one piece, not the whole "get found → get answered → get booked → get followed up" chain.
**What it costs them:** Direct lost jobs/revenue per missed call; slow decay of Google ranking/reviews from an unmaintained profile; owner's own time if they try to do outreach/GBP posts/review replies themselves.
**Emotional tension:** Guilt/stress about being unreachable while working; distrust of "more marketing spend" pitches without proof; skepticism that an AI voice will sound real or handle their specific trade correctly.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Real AI voice conversation (Gemini Live, not a script/IVR tree) that qualifies, answers business-specific questions, and books a real calendar slot
- Website + AI reception + local marketing (GBP, reviews, reporting) bundled as one flat price, not stitched together from 3+ vendors
- Website built and live same day (~6 hours, no human involved) — most competitors quote weeks
- Flat $297/mo, no per-minute/per-call metering — cost is predictable regardless of call volume
**How we do it differently:** Full pipeline (lead discovery → site build → AI reception → SMS/email confirmation loop → local marketing) is automated end-to-end rather than a single point tool.
**Why that's better:** Owner does zero setup work beyond a short onboarding, and the cost doesn't scale against them as their call volume grows (unlike per-minute answering services).
**Why customers choose us:** Speed (site live overnight), price predictability (flat, not metered), and being able to test the live AI reception line themselves before buying — proof before commitment.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** A missed call that visibly became a lost job; a slow/ugly existing site; falling Google ranking from a neglected GBP profile.
**Pull:** Free demo site or live test call with zero commitment; $0 setup + 30-day trial removes upfront risk; "wake up to a text with your live site" is a fast, tangible first win.
**Habit:** Owner has "always just dealt with" missed calls manually, or has an existing (even if mediocre) website/vendor relationship they haven't gotten around to replacing.
**Anxiety:** Will the AI actually represent my business correctly on a real call; will switching disrupt the phone number/existing workflow (FAQ directly answers this: "No, we work with your existing business flow").
